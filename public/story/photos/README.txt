Original photos for "Our First Year"
====================================

Drop real photographs in THIS folder. The film picks them up automatically;
anything missing just shows an empty frame, so the film always runs.

Use these exact names (any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp):

  gatherings.jpg   home & library gatherings
  easter.jpg       the Easter egg hunt
  circle.jpg       Circle of Friends
  parksrec.jpg     the Parks & Rec event
  softball.jpg     a softball session
  family.jpg       spare slot — a family photo
  aaria.jpg        spare slot — Aaria

Notes
-----
· Landscape works best. Roughly 1600x1250 or larger; they are cropped to fill
  a polaroid frame (4:3-ish) with a slow push-in.
· These are real children. Only use photos you have permission to publish, and
  keep the folder out of anything public until you are happy with each one.
· The frames live in chapter 11 ("The first year"). To move a photo to another
  chapter, pass its key to S.photo('key') inside that chapter in scenes.js.
